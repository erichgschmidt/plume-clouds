Field
Canvas
Paint fixes primary positions; billows and noise still animate. Erase removes whole masses. Size is the brush radius as % of canvas width. B / E / L select tools; [ ] resize.
Simulation
Shape turbulence
Enabled and Flow apply to both noise layers. Inflation, Billow and Embed remain available with noise disabled.
Primary shapes
Independent noise for the large forms. Higher Scale makes finer breakup. Strength is compensated around neutral size; Inflation adds fullness to the whole parcel.
Secondary billows
Billow grows the puffs. Embed moves their round centers into the primary: 0 is exposed, 1 fully buried. Noise, Strength and Scale affect only these smaller forms.
Look
AO finds local nooks. Radius sets their scale; higher Falloff keeps the shading closer to enclosing surfaces. Intensity changes their depth.
Toon shading
Tone A sets the bright cutoff. Tone B sets the shade cutoff. Ink emphasizes outlines and exposed edges.
