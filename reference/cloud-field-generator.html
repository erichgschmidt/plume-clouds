How this field works
Moving groups define the silhouette. One broad depth surface is reconstructed across every connected shape, then a restrained layer of positive billow relief is added. The light follows the whole form.
The starting appearance restores the original shared-depth study: 35% billow relief, 40% secondary field, 18 lighting steps, sun 120°, and light toward the viewer 17°. Small patches enrich the broad planes without giving every puff its own lighting.
The motion remains a deterministic loop: tufts leave a body, drift, and merge into another. This is a fixed-view 2.5D field with shallow surface shadows.
Space play / pause   R restart   F fullscreen. Drag the preview to move the sun. Scrubbing pauses the animation.
Use Split / merge to watch a tuft cross open sky. Reset to reference appearance restores the lighting and surface settings while retaining your seed, composition, and motion settings.

 




The shared-depth reference
Broad connected forms. Quiet blue shadow planes. Restrained relief and a muted cream-to-cyan palette.
This is the appearance guiding the generator. The moving shapes are rendered from one shared broad depth field before lighting.
